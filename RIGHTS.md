# Rights and source attribution

The raw NHIS files and official methodological documents originate from the
US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Center for Health
Statistics. Their original attribution and applicable data-use notice are retained.

Source: <https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhis/documentation/2025-nhis.html>

This repository does not claim ownership of CDC/NCHS materials, assign a new
license to them, or imply CDC/NCHS review or endorsement of the analyses.

No project-wide license has been assigned to the newly written analysis code,
figures, literature synthesis or manuscript. Any future project license should
clearly distinguish those original contributions from third-party materials.

Bibliographic records and source links document the literature search. The
repository should not contain publisher full texts unless redistribution is
permitted; a linked or locally managed Zotero attachment is not automatically
permission to redistribute the article.
