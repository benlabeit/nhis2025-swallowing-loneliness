The NHIS public-use data and official methodological documents originate from the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Center for Health Statistics.

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhis/documentation/2025-nhis.html

This repository does not claim ownership of CDC/NCHS materials, apply a new license to them, or imply CDC/NCHS review or endorsement of the analyses.

The NCHS Data User Agreement applies:
https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html

Data are used for statistical reporting and analysis. No attempt should be made to identify respondents or link the public-use records to individually identifiable records.

No project-wide license has been assigned to the manuscript, original analysis code or figures. Bibliographic citations and source links identify published literature; publisher full texts are not distributed with these materials.
